# Project Governance

The engineering of **Aipo** is governed by formal quality assurance processes, clean architecture boundaries, and full requirement traceability.

---

## Governance Pillars

- **[Prumo CLI & LPC Methodology](/en/governance/prumo-and-lpc)**: Lifecycle management driven by Prumo CLI and the *Lean Progressive Context* paradigm (smallest sufficient context, progressive expansion, pointer over payload).
- **[Code Standards & Testing Strategy](/en/governance/standards-and-testing)**: Rust 2024 coding guidelines, testing pyramid (unit, property, integration, conformance, differential, and fuzzing), and automated quality gates.
- **[Security & Sandboxing](/en/governance/security-and-threat-model)**: Security charter, threat modeling against Denial-of-Service, parser stack recursion depth caps, and sandboxed Host ABI capability trees.
- **[Living Changelog](/en/governance/changelog)**: Comprehensive log of versions, releases, delivered slices, and architectural evolutions.
