# Evidence & Performance Protocol

Aipo engineering is governed by the core principle of **Evidence-Based Engineering**: no claims of speed, safety, or conformance are accepted without measurable metrics, reproducible test fixtures, and automated validation scripts.

---

## Evidence Sections

- **[Cross-Language Benchmarks](/en/evidence/cross-language)**: Standardized execution benchmarks comparing Aipo CLI/VM, in-process Aipo, Aipo transpiled to JavaScript (Node.js), Lua, LuaJIT, Wren, Luau, CPython, PyPy, Ruby, and native Rust.
- **[The Optimization Saga & Lessons](/en/evidence/performance-lessons)**: An honest, rigorous post-mortem of 7 micro-optimization experiments reverted due to lack of demonstrated speedup, VM dispatch floor analysis, and measurement discipline under CPU contention.
- **[Conformance Matrix](/en/evidence/conformance)**: Over 40 test suites covering canonical programs, semantic diagnostics, and bit-for-bit VM ↔ JS differential parity.
- **[Slice History](/en/evidence/slices)**: Comprehensive record of certification reports across every vertical slice (P00-G01 through P04-G11).
